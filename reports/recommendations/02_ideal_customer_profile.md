# Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)
### Who to acquire more of, and who to protect

**Method:** the ICP is derived empirically from the two highest-quality personas
— **Premium Loyalists** and **Loyal Full-Price** customers (n ≈ 639) — not from
intuition. These are the customers who combine high projected value, high
loyalty, **low discount reliance**, and high satisfaction. Profile figures below
come directly from the feature table.

---

## 1. The ICP at a glance

> **A ~45-year-old repeat apparel buyer who shops weekly, pays full price, rates
> us ~4.4/5, and buys core wardrobe items — boots, coats, skirts, jewelry —
> across the South-East and North-East. Average projected CLV ≈ $7,145, roughly
> 2.2× the overall average of ~$3,230.**

## 2. Profile detail

| Dimension | ICP signature | Read-out |
|---|---|---|
| **Age** | Median 45 (IQR 31–57) | Established, higher-disposable-income adult — not the youngest cohort. |
| **Purchase cadence** | Predominantly **Weekly / Bi-Weekly** | High velocity is the strongest loyalty signal we have. |
| **Category** | Clothing (42%), Accessories (30%), Footwear (18%) | Core apparel, not one-off niche buys. |
| **Hero items** | Boots, Coats, Skirts, Jewelry | Wardrobe staples + considered accessories. |
| **Satisfaction** | Avg rating **4.39** | Materially above the 3.75 base average. |
| **Discount behaviour** | Low promo dependency by construction | They buy because they want to, not because it's cheap. |
| **Geography** | New York, Georgia, Tennessee, Alabama, Maryland | A South-East + North-East spine. |
| **Shipping** | Skews to **2-Day / Express / Next-Day** | Willing to pay for speed — premium intent. |
| **Payment** | Credit Card, PayPal, Cash mix | No single lock-in; convenience-led. |

## 3. Why leadership should care

- **Acquisition efficiency:** every dollar spent acquiring an ICP look-alike
  returns ~2.2× the average customer's projected value — and does so **without a
  discount subsidy**, so the margin is real.
- **Retention priority:** this cohort is the core the Promotional Sunset Plan is
  designed to *protect*. They must never be the target of price-led messaging.
- **Creative & merchandising direction:** lead acquisition creative with the hero
  items (boots, coats) and the speed-of-delivery value proposition, not price.

## 4. Anti-profile (who NOT to chase)

The **Promo Hunter** persona (n ≈ 1,047; avg CLV ≈ $814) is the mirror image:
discount-dependent, low value, low loyalty. Acquisition campaigns that lead with
discounts disproportionately attract this cohort — **growth that dilutes margin**.
Suppress discount-led creative in prospecting to avoid manufacturing more of them.

## 5. How to operationalise the ICP

1. **Look-alike acquisition:** export the ICP cohort (`customer_persona IN
   ('Premium Loyalist','Loyal Full-Price')`) and seed look-alike audiences on
   paid social / programmatic.
2. **Geo-weighting:** over-index spend on the ICP's strong states; test adjacent
   markets with similar demographics.
3. **Creative:** wardrobe-staple hero products + speed/service value prop;
   **no discount in the hook**.
4. **Onboarding:** route new ICP-matched customers into the loyalty programme
   immediately to lock in full-price habit before any discount exposure.

> **Caveat:** gender is confounded with discounting in the source data, so the
> ICP intentionally does **not** target on gender. Validate any demographic
> targeting against live campaign response.
