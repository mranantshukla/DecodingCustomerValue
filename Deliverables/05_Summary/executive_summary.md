## Data-integrity caveats

- **Subscription–discount confound:** every subscriber in the source data carries a discount. 'Subscribers are discount-dependent' is therefore a data artefact, not a behavioural finding — promo metrics are reported net of subscription where it matters.
- **Gender–discount confound:** no female customer in the source data received a discount. Geographic/segment discount rates partly reflect gender mix, not pure regional behaviour; treat promo-by-geography as directional and validate before acting.
- **No timestamps / churn labels:** loyalty, tenure, and CLV are engineered proxies built from `previous_purchases` (volume) and `frequency_of_purchases` (cadence). They rank customers reliably but are not audited financial figures.

## So what (top 3 moves)

1. **Protect the top 20%.** They carry 59.5% of projected value — a retention failure here dwarfs any acquisition win. Ring-fence them from discount-led messaging.
2. **Sunset discounts where they are habit, not stimulus.** 1,326 customers are discount-dependent; the promotional sunset plan sequences who loses discounts first by margin-at-risk vs retention-risk.
3. **Reallocate to organic-demand regions.** Region quality and organic demand scores identify markets that grow without subsidy — the highest ROI destination for freed-up promotional budget.
